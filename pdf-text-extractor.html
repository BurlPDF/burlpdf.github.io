How it works

  1. Add your PDF. Drag it onto the box above or pick it from your device.
  2. Choose what to extract. Use every page, or type a range such as 1-5 or 2, 4, 7-9.
  3. Extract and save. Copy the text or download it as a .txt file.

The PDF is read by pdf.js inside your browser tab. Nothing is sent to a server.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is processed entirely on your device. Only the pdf.js library itself is downloaded from a CDN when the page loads.

Why is the result empty for my PDF?

Scanned PDFs and photos of documents contain images of text, not real text. This tool reads embedded text only, so those files need OCR software first.

Can I extract only certain pages?

Yes. Enter pages and ranges separated by commas, like 1-3, 7, 10-12. Leave the field blank to extract everything.

Why is the line spacing or order a little off?

PDFs store text as positioned fragments rather than paragraphs. The tool rebuilds lines from those positions, which works well for most documents but can be imperfect with columns and tables.

Does it work with password-protected PDFs?

Files that need a password to open are not supported. Remove the password first, then try again.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit. Very large PDFs depend on your device's memory and may take longer to process.
