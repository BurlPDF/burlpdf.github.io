How it works

1. Add your PDF

Drag a file onto the page or pick one. It is opened by your browser only, with no upload.

2. Set up your text

Type the text, choose font, size, color, opacity and placement, then pick all pages or specific ones. The preview updates as you type.

3. Download

Press Apply & Download. A new copy is created on your device and your original file stays untouched.

FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Reading, previewing and editing all happen in your browser using JavaScript libraries. Nothing is sent to a server, so confidential documents stay on your device.

How do I make a diagonal watermark?

Click the Diagonal preset. It centers your text at a 45° angle. Lower the opacity to around 20–30% so the page content stays readable.

Can I stamp only certain pages?

Yes. Choose Specific pages and enter numbers or ranges such as 1, 3-5, 9. In that mode you can also click thumbnails to select or deselect pages.

Why are some characters rejected?

The built-in PDF fonts support Latin characters (English and most Western European languages). Symbols outside that set, such as Chinese, Arabic or emoji, cannot be drawn with these fonts.

Does this change the existing text in my PDF?

No. It adds new text on top of the pages. Existing content is not edited or removed.

Why does it say my PDF is protected?

Password-protected PDFs cannot be modified here. Remove the password first, then try again.
