      FAQ

      Are my files uploaded anywhere?

      No. The ZIP library runs entirely in your browser, so your files stay on your device. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it will keep working.

      Is there a file size limit?

      There is no fixed limit, but your browser has to hold everything in memory. Archives of a few hundred megabytes usually work well. Very large files may be slow or fail on phones and older computers.

      Why did my browser ask to allow multiple downloads?

      "Download All Files" saves each extracted file separately, so browsers may ask for permission to download multiple files. Choose Allow to receive them all.

      Can I open password-protected ZIP files?

      No. Encrypted archives aren't supported. Remove the password with the tool that created the archive first.

      Are folders kept inside the ZIP?

      When you create a ZIP, files are added at the top level of the archive. When you extract, the list shows each file's full path inside the archive, and downloaded files are saved by their file name.

      Files are processed locally and never leave your browser.
