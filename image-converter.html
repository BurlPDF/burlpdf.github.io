    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded anywhere?

    No. Conversion happens on your device using your browser’s canvas. Your files never leave your computer or phone, and nothing is stored once you close the page.

    What happens to transparency?

    PNG and WEBP keep transparent areas. JPG and BMP don’t support transparency here, so transparent areas are filled with white.

    Which quality setting should I use?

    Around 80–90% is a good balance for photos: the file gets much smaller and the difference is hard to see. Use 100% if you need the best possible result. Quality only applies to JPG and WEBP.

    Why can’t I convert to WEBP in my browser?

    Some browsers, mainly older versions of Safari, can open WEBP images but can’t create them. If you see an error, try a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, or choose another format.

    Is there a limit on how many images I can convert?

    There’s no fixed limit. Very large images or very large batches use your device’s memory, so if things slow down, convert in smaller groups.

    Will converting change my original files?

    No. Your originals stay exactly as they are. You download new files with the same name and the new extension.
