Frequently asked questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The QR code is drawn by JavaScript running in your browser. Your text, Wi-Fi password and UPI details are never sent to a server. The only thing fetched from the internet is the small QR library when the page loads.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. The data is stored directly inside the image, so the code works for as long as the content it points to stays valid. There are no accounts, limits or tracking links.

Will a Wi-Fi QR code work on my phone?

Yes. The camera on current iPhones and most Android phones can scan a Wi-Fi code and offer to join the network. Make sure the security type matches your router settings.

Can I use the UPI code to receive payments?

Yes. Scanning it opens a UPI app with your UPI ID and name filled in. If you add an amount, it is pre-filled for the payer. Test with a small payment before printing it for customers.

Which colors scan best?

Use a dark foreground on a light background, such as black on white. Inverted codes (light on dark) and low-contrast colors may fail on some scanners.

What does error correction do?

Higher levels let the code stay readable even if part of it is smudged or covered, but they make the pattern denser. Medium suits most uses. Choose High for printed codes that may get worn.
