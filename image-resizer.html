Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. The resize runs on your device using your browser’s Canvas API. Your image is never sent anywhere, and it works even if you go offline after the page has loaded.

What does “Lock aspect ratio” do?

It keeps the width and height in proportion. When you change one, the other updates automatically so the image is not stretched or squashed. Turn it off if you need an exact width and height that differ from the original proportions.

Which format and quality should I choose?

Use JPG for photos, PNG for graphics, screenshots and anything with transparency, and WEBP for the smallest files on the web. Quality applies to JPG and WEBP only; 80–90% is a good balance of size and sharpness. PNG is lossless, so it has no quality setting.

What happens to transparent areas when I save as JPG?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. Choose PNG or WEBP to keep transparency.

Why is my resized file larger than the original?

Making an image bigger adds pixels, which usually means more data. Switching from JPG to PNG can also increase the size. Try WEBP or JPG with a lower quality to shrink the file.

Is there a size limit?

There is no file limit from us, but browsers cap canvas size. Output is limited to 16,384 px per side and about 268 million pixels in total. Very large images may be slow on older phones.
