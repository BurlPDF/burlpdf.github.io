How it works

  1. Upload a PDF by dropping it on the box above or choosing it from your device.
  2. Type the pages you want, such as 1, 3, 5-7, or use a quick button.
  3. Select Extract & Download PDF. A new PDF with only those pages is saved to your device.
Your PDF is processed entirely in your browser using the open-source pdf-lib library. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The file is read and rebuilt on your own device. It never leaves your browser, and it works even if you go offline after the page has loaded.

Can I choose pages in a different order?

Yes. Pages are added to the new PDF in the order you type them. Entering 5, 1, 3 gives you page 5 first, then page 1, then page 3. Repeated pages are only included once.

Does the original file change?

No. Your original PDF stays exactly as it was. You get a separate new file named after the original with “-extracted” added.

Why can’t I open my PDF?

Password-protected or damaged PDFs can’t be read. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then try again.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit. Because everything runs in your browser’s memory, very large files (hundreds of MB) may be slow on older devices.
