Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is read and re-encoded by your browser using an HTML5 canvas. It never touches a server, and it works even if you go offline after the page loads.

What quality setting should I use?

For photos, 70–80% usually looks nearly identical to the original at a much smaller size. Go lower for thumbnails or email attachments, and higher when fine detail matters.

Why does my PNG turn into a WEBP?

PNG is a lossless format, and browsers ignore the quality setting when saving one. To give you real savings, PNG files are converted to WEBP by default. WEBP also keeps transparency. You can choose JPG instead, but transparent areas will become white.

Why is the compressed file sometimes larger?

If the original was already heavily optimized, re-encoding it can add size. Lower the quality, or try another output format. If nothing helps, keep your original.

Does it change the image dimensions or remove metadata?

Width and height stay exactly the same. Metadata such as camera info and GPS location isn’t carried over, which is a small privacy bonus.
