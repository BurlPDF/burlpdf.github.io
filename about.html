About This Toolset

A collection of PDF, image, and data utilities that run entirely on your device. Your files are opened, processed, and saved in your browser, and they are never sent to a server.

Browse all tools See how it works

Overview

This toolset brings everyday file utilities into one place: working with PDFs, editing and converting images, and cleaning up data and text. Each tool is built with standard web technologies and runs inside the browser tab you already have open.

Most online file tools work by uploading your document to a remote server, processing it there, and sending a result back. That means your contracts, scans, photos, and spreadsheets pass through infrastructure you do not control. Here, the processing happens locally instead, so there is no upload step and no copy of your file stored elsewhere.

Running locally is also simpler to trust. If a tool never receives your file, it cannot leak, log, or keep it.

Key highlights

🔒

No uploads to external servers

Files are read directly from your device and never leave it. There are no accounts, no file queues, and nothing to delete afterwards.

With no upload or download wait, results appear as fast as your device can compute them, even on slow or unstable connections.

How the client-side architecture works

The whole site is static. The pages and scripts are delivered to your browser once, and from then on every operation happens on your device.

  1. 1
    You choose a fileThe browser gives the page access to the file you selected or dropped. The file stays on your device.
  2. 2
    Your browser processes itJavaScript running in the tab reads and transforms the file in memory, using your device's own processor.
  3. 3
    You save the resultThe finished file is generated locally and downloaded straight to your device.
Good to know: Because everything happens in your browser, closing the tab clears the working data from memory. Very large files depend on your device's available memory, so a phone may handle them more slowly than a computer.
