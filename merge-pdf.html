    Frequently asked questions

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. The merge runs entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib library. Your PDFs never leave your device, and nothing is sent to a server.

    Is there a limit on file size or number of files?

    There's no fixed limit. Because everything runs on your device, very large files or very many files depend on your computer's available memory.

    Why did one of my files fail to merge?

    Password-protected or damaged PDFs can't be read. Remove the password in a PDF viewer, save a fresh copy, and add it again.

    Will the merged PDF keep my original pages?

    Yes. Every page is copied in full, in the order your files are listed. Text, images and page sizes stay as they were. Interactive extras such as bookmarks and form fields may not carry over.

    Does it work offline?

    Once this page has loaded, merging doesn't need a connection. You do need to be online the first time so the pdf-lib library can load.
