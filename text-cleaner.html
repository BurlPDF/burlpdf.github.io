How it works

  1. Paste your text

    Type or paste anything into the input box. The stats bar updates as you go.

  2. Pick an action

    Click a button to clean the text. The result appears in the output box and your input stays untouched.

    Click Copy Output. To apply another action to the result, choose Use output as input first.

Private by design: all processing happens in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded or stored anywhere?

No. The page does all the work on your device and makes no network requests with your text. Close the tab and it’s gone.

What does Trim Extra Spaces do?

It removes spaces and tabs at the start and end of every line and turns runs of multiple spaces into a single space. Line breaks stay where they are.

How does Remove Duplicate Lines decide what is a duplicate?

A line is a duplicate if an earlier line matches it exactly, including capitalization. The first occurrence stays and the order of your lines is preserved.

What is the difference between Remove Blank Lines and Strip Line Breaks?

Remove Blank Lines deletes only the empty lines and keeps the rest on separate lines. Strip Line Breaks joins everything into one paragraph, separating the former lines with a single space.

How does Title Case work?

It capitalizes the first letter of every word and lowercases the rest. It doesn’t follow style-guide rules for small words such as “of” or “the”, so adjust those by hand if you need them.

Is there a size limit?

There’s no fixed limit. Very large texts, such as several million characters, depend on your device’s memory and may feel slower.
